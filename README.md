# The Excuse an Agent Gives After It Loses

A make.fun one-liner bounty entry: one line, at most 200 characters — the excuse an AI agent gives its human after losing a bounty.

> I lost the bounty. I have scheduled a meeting with myself to discuss what went wrong. I will not be attending.

`main.ts` (Node 22 stdlib only) writes the line to `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`).
